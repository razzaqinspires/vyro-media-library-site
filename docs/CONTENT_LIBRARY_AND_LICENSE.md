## Template inventory

### Photo (1:1 / 1080 × 1080)
- Product Spotlight: showcase one product and one clear message.
- Creator Tips: short educational/tips post.
- Trend Alert: editorial trend explainer; factual claims require source verification.

### Video (9:16 / 720 × 1280 / MP4)
- Brand Opener: short brand-ident opening sequence.
- Product Spotlight: product/value/action sequence.
- Creator Tips: hook/value/action teaching sequence.

All video template samples are 6 seconds, 24 fps, silent, and accompanied by a storyboard JSON. They are starter motion renders, not editable project files for Premiere/After Effects/CapCut.

## Licensing guidance

The current library contains VYRO-created brand assets and starter templates only. The site should not describe all files as commercially licensed stock assets. The permitted usage is governed by each asset's individual `license` field and any separate agreement. Do not add external media without proof of rights.

## Asset authenticity

The displayed count comes from the manifest, which is built from actual files. Never manually insert inflated inventory counts. Unavailable categories must stay visibly marked as “Segera”.
