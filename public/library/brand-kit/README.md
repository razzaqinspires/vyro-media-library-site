# VYRO MEDIA — Brand Identity Kit v1.0

**Date:** 23 November 2026  
**Instagram:** @vyromedia.id  
**Tagline:** Make Attention Matter.  
**Brand scope:** Independent brand. This kit has no affiliation with Razzaq Ventures or any prior business.

## Package contents

- `01_Logo/01_Symbol_Only/` — three square icon-only logo variants: black, white, and neon-lime backgrounds.
- `01_Logo/02_Full_Logo/` — three square full lockups including VYRO MEDIA, descriptor, and tagline.
- `01_Logo/03_Transparent/` — transparent symbol and full lockup PNGs for layout work.
- `02_Profile_Name_Bio/` — three square social profile concept cards with display-name and bio directions.
- `03_Banners/01_Plain/` — three square background-only brand panels.
- `03_Banners/02_With_Name/` — three square brand-name banners.
- `03_Banners/03_With_Information/` — three square service-information banners.
- `04_Instagram_Highlights/` — the 12 previously prepared Instagram highlight cover PNGs.
- `05_Documentation/` — design system, copy options, file inventory, and production notes.
- `06_Preview/` — contact sheets for quick review.

All newly generated social artwork is **1080 × 1080 px (1:1)** unless otherwise noted.

- **Instagram avatar:** use `VYRO_Symbol_Black_1080.png` first. The white-background version is the light-mode alternative; the lime-background version is best used for campaign or temporary profile experiments.

The three profile concepts are alternative presentation directions, not a recommendation to rename the brand every week. Keep the handle `@vyromedia.id` consistent.
