# VYRO MEDIA — Design System & Brand Usage Guide

## 1. Brand foundation

**Name:** VYRO MEDIA  
**Handle:** @vyromedia.id  
**Tagline:** Make Attention Matter.  
**Descriptor:** Creator • Content • Promotion  
**Positioning:** an independent digital media and creative-content brand combining original trend-led content, short-form production, creator collaboration, UGC, and promotional campaign services.

VYRO MEDIA should feel direct, contemporary, energetic, and commercially useful. The brand should look like a real media and creative operation—not a generic repost account. Use original commentary, recorded footage, licensed materials, and clear campaign disclosures.

## 2. Brand color system

| Token | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| VYRO Black | `#050709` | Primary dark background, premium layouts |
| VYRO White | `#F7F9F6` | Light background, negative space |
| VYRO Neon Lime | `#B8FF00` | Primary accent, highlights, CTA emphasis |
| VYRO Dark | `#0D1116` | Text and dark marks on light backgrounds |
| Neutral Gray | `#5A6267` | Secondary copy on light surfaces |

### Background variants

1. **Black:** primary and most recognisable. White symbol with lime accent.
2. **White:** clean and corporate. Dark symbol with lime accent.
3. **Neon Lime:** campaign/attention variant. Dark symbol with white accent.

Do not use the same black background for every output. Use the white variant for clear contrast and the lime variant selectively, because it is visually strong.

## 3. Typography

- **Display/headlines:** Inter Display Bold or Inter ExtraBold.
- **Body/captions:** Inter Regular or Inter Medium.
- **Buttons/labels:** Inter Bold.
- **Fallback:** system sans-serif with comparable proportions.

Keep headlines short. Avoid thin fonts on neon backgrounds. Do not use more than two type families in one composition. Font files are not included in this package; use properly licensed font sources.

## 4. Logo system

- **Symbol-only:** avatar, highlight cover branding, watermark, favicon.
- **Full lockup:** profile cards, campaign artwork, service sheets, and banners.
- **Transparent PNG:** place over approved brand backgrounds.

### Clear space and sizing

- Maintain clear space around the symbol of at least 10% of the artwork width.
- Keep the symbol centered and preserve its aspect ratio.
- For Instagram avatars, keep the symbol comfortably inside the central 80% circle-safe area.
- Do not stretch, skew, redraw, crop the star/swoosh, or add unofficial effects.
- Do not place lime symbol elements directly on lime backgrounds without the alternate dark/white treatment.

## 6. Banner system

All banner artwork in this delivery is 1:1 as requested. These are square social tiles, not traditional wide headers.

- **Plain banners:** reusable background panels for future layouts.
- **Name banners:** brand awareness and pinned-post tiles.
- **Information banners:** service explanation, carousel cover, or pinned service tiles.

For Facebook Page cover, website hero, or YouTube channel art, create a separate wide adaptation instead of stretching the square assets.

## 7. Content and service language

Approved service labels for early-stage use:

- Short-form video production
- UGC and product content
- Video editing and creative concepts
- Creator discovery and talent coordination
- Brand collaboration
- Promotion and campaign content

Do not promise FYP, guaranteed reach, follower growth, sales, or virality. Quote a deliverable, format, revision count, deadline, usage rights, and fee in each client agreement.

## 8. Content integrity and permissions

- Prefer original footage, original scripts, and client-authorised material.
- Obtain permission and agreed usage scope before publishing a client's product, face, voice, or testimonial.
- Obtain written consent from creators/talents, including usage period, platforms, paid-ad usage, exclusivity, fee, and cancellation terms.
- Label paid promotions, gifts, affiliate content, and brand partnerships clearly where required by platform rules and applicable law.
- Do not reuse third-party videos with only minor edits as a growth strategy.
- Keep records of licences, releases, invoices, and campaign approvals.

## 9. Export and publishing standards

- Social square artwork: PNG, 1080 × 1080 px, sRGB.
- Instagram avatar: upload symbol-only square; check circular crop on mobile.
- Reels/TikTok video: 1080 × 1920 px, 9:16, with captions inside safe margins.
- Keep important text away from outer edges. Preview every upload on a phone before publishing.
